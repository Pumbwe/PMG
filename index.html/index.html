Industrial Chemistry — 2nd Year

Industrial track courses are grouped separately. Each course page contains Past Papers, Notes, and Assignments. Files are expected under industrial_chemistry/.

Structure, reactivity and synthesis of organic compounds.

CH210 — Industrial Chemistry

Large-scale chemical processes and plant operations.

Quantitative and qualitative analysis techniques.

MA210 — Industrial Mathematics

Mathematical methods used in industrial process modelling.

PH110 — Industrial Physics

Applied physics topics relevant to industrial settings.

How to use

Click a course's Open button to load its course page into the viewer below. Course pages follow the convention:

  • NON_QUOTA/notes/<code>.html for non-quota courses
  • industrial_chemistry/<code>.html for industrial courses
  • Past papers may also be stored under files/ as files/<year>_<code>.html
