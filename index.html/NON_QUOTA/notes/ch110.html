CH110 Chemistry Notes

1. Introduction to Chemistry

Chemistry is the scientific study of matter, its properties, and its interactions with other forms of matter.

2. Basic Concepts

Atoms are the basic building blocks of matter. They consist of protons, neutrons, and electrons.

3. Chemical Reactions

In a chemical reaction, substances (reactants) transform into different substances (products).

4. Key Terms

  • Element: A pure substance made of only one type of atom.
  • Compound: A substance made of two or more different types of atoms chemically bonded together.
  • Mixture: A combination of two or more substances that are not chemically bonded.
