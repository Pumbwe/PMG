Curved Arrows Formalism and Bond Fission Mechanisms

Curved Arrows Formalism

The curved arrows formalism is a crucial tool in organic chemistry for depicting the movement of electrons during chemical reactions. Curved arrows indicate the direction of electron flow, helping to visualize the formation and breaking of bonds.

Homolytic Fission

Homolytic fission refers to the process where a covalent bond breaks symmetrically, resulting in the formation of two radicals. Each atom retains one of the electrons from the bond, which can lead to various radical reactions in organic molecules.

Heterolytic Fission

Heterolytic fission involves the cleavage of a covalent bond where one atom retains both bonding electrons, resulting in the formation of a cation and an anion. This mechanism is essential for understanding ionic reactions and the behavior of electrophiles and nucleophiles.
