First Year NQ — Copperbelt University

This program introduces students to foundational courses in Chemistry and Mathematics, preparing them for advanced study in science and engineering disciplines.

About the Program

The First Year NQ curriculum at Copperbelt University emphasizes:

  • CH110 — Chemistry: Atomic structure, bonding, states of matter, and introductory organic chemistry.
  • MA110 — Mathematics: Calculus, algebra, and problem-solving techniques for scientific applications.

Students gain a strong foundation in theoretical concepts and practical problem-solving, setting the stage for specialized courses in later years.
