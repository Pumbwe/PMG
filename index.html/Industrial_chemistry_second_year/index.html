Industrial Chemistry Second Year

This program focuses on advanced concepts in organic chemistry, analytical techniques, and applied industrial processes. Students gain practical skills through lab work, assignments, and exam practice.

About the Program

Industrial Chemistry Second Year builds on the fundamentals of chemistry with emphasis on:

  • Organic Chemistry (CH260) — hybridization, aromaticity, reaction mechanisms
  • Analytical Chemistry — quantitative and qualitative analysis methods
  • Industrial processes — chemical engineering principles applied to production

The curriculum combines theory with practice, preparing students for both academic progression and industry roles.
