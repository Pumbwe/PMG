Quick study notes

Delocalization: π electrons spread across atoms stabilize molecules; benzene is a classic example.

Inductive effect: Electron withdrawal or donation through σ bonds; affects acidity and reactivity.

Hyperconjugation: Stabilization by overlap of σ bonds with adjacent empty orbitals; explains carbocation stability.

Example: Tertiary carbocations are more stable than primary due to hyperconjugation and inductive donation.

Analytical Chemistry summary

Analytical chemistry measures what is in a sample and how much. It includes qualitative tests (what) and quantitative methods (how much) using classical and instrumental techniques.

Example: Use a UV‑Vis calibration curve to determine concentration from absorbance.
