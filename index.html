Organic Chemistry

Organic Chemistry Notes (CH 260)

What is Organic Chemistry?
It is the study of carbon compounds — their structure, properties, reactions, and uses. Carbon is unique because it can form strong bonds with itself and other elements, and it makes four covalent bonds, giving rise to millions of compounds.

Why It Matters

  • Basis of life: all living things are made of organic compounds.
  • Applications: plastics, fuels, medicines, electronics, energy storage.

Course Outline (Simplified)

  1. Classification: Sources, naming rules, importance.
  2. Structure & Bonding: Hybridization, how atoms connect.
  3. Reaction Mechanisms: Using arrows to show electron movement.
  4. Reagents: Electrophiles (electron-loving) & nucleophiles (nucleus-loving).
  5. Stereochemistry I: Isomerism, chirality (mirror-image molecules).
  6. Stereochemistry II: E/Z naming system.
  7. Alkanes & Cycloalkanes: Naming, preparation, reactions.
  8. Arenes: Benzene and aromatic compounds.
  9. Alkenes: Naming, formation, E1/E2/E1cb reaction mechanisms.
  10. Dienes & Alkynes: Structures and chemical behavior.
  11. Halides: Alkyl and aryl halides, preparation and reactions.
  12. Alcohols: Types, naming, preparation from other compounds.

Study Tips

  • Read before class and note main ideas.
  • Summarize lectures within 24 hours.
  • Practice problems independently.
  • Review daily to avoid cramming.
